MYBIE SWORD

Official Brand Concept & Project Philosophy

One House. Multiple Sword Coins. One Standard.

1. Introduction

MYBIE SWORD is an independent umbrella brand established to bring together multiple cryptocurrency projects built around a shared sword-themed identity and philosophy.

MYBIE SWORD is not a single cryptocurrency. It is the overarching brand that provides a common identity, direction, and set of principles for the projects developed under its name.

Each project may have its own identity and characteristics while remaining aligned with the core values of MYBIE SWORD.

2. The Meaning of the Sword

The sword is the central symbol of MYBIE SWORD.

It represents:


Protection — standing for what matters rather than seeking destruction.


Courage — facing uncertainty and challenges.


Integrity — remaining committed to principles.


Honor — acting with responsibility and respect.


Resilience — strength shaped through challenges and experience.

The sword was never intended to symbolize aggression or harm. It represents the determination to protect hope, uphold principles, and face chaos with courage.

3. The First Project: $MYBIE

$MYBIE is the first cryptocurrency project developed under the MYBIE SWORD brand.

It represents the starting point of the brand's journey and serves as the primary focus during its initial development stage.

The immediate priority is to establish a solid foundation for $MYBIE through responsible project development, community building, transparency, and security awareness.

Future projects will only be considered when the first project has developed a sufficient foundation.

4. Blockchain Strategy

Solana is the initial blockchain network selected for the launch of $MYBIE.

MYBIE SWORD maintains a flexible approach to future technological development. Network decisions for future projects will depend on technical requirements, available resources, security considerations, and project readiness.

Developing an independent blockchain network is a long-term ambition, not a current launch commitment. It will only be considered if the necessary technical capabilities and resources become available.

5. Core Standards

Projects operating under the MYBIE SWORD brand are expected to uphold the following principles:

Security

Prioritize responsible security practices and protect project infrastructure and community members.

Transparency

Communicate project information clearly and provide verifiable information whenever possible.

Honesty

Avoid misleading statements, unrealistic promises, and unsupported claims.

Responsibility

Make decisions with consideration for the project, its community, and its long-term sustainability.

Accountability

Maintain appropriate documentation and make important project decisions traceable.

These principles are standards the brand aims to uphold. They do not eliminate the risks associated with cryptocurrency or guarantee financial outcomes.

6. Brand Structure

The overall structure is:

MYBIE SWORD — Umbrella Brand


$MYBIE — First cryptocurrency project and current primary focus.


Future Sword-Themed Projects — Potential future projects developed under the same brand standards.

Each future project will be evaluated independently before development or release.

7. Long-Term Vision

MYBIE SWORD aims to grow into a recognizable brand with a consistent identity, responsible project standards, and a community built around shared principles.

The long-term vision is to expand carefully, strengthen the technological foundation when appropriate, and preserve the identity of the brand as it develops.

Growth will not be pursued at the expense of security, transparency, or responsibility.

8. Closing Statement

MYBIE SWORD is built around more than a visual symbol.

The sword represents the principles that guide the brand: protection, courage, integrity, honor, and resilience.

Every project under this name should reflect those principles through its actions, communication, and development.
