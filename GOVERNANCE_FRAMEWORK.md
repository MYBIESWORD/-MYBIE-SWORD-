MYBIE SWORD — GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

1. Purpose

This document establishes the governance framework for the MYBIE SWORD ecosystem.

Its purpose is to define decision-making principles, authority boundaries, accountability, and operational standards as the ecosystem develops.

Good governance is essential for maintaining trust, consistency, and long-term sustainability.

2. Governance Principles

MYBIE SWORD governance is based on five fundamental principles:

- Security: Protect project assets, systems, accounts, and community interests.
- Transparency: Communicate relevant decisions and developments clearly.
- Honesty: Provide accurate information and avoid misleading claims.
- Responsibility: Ensure decisions are made with appropriate consideration of their consequences.
- Accountability: Make responsibilities and decision ownership clear.

3. Governance Structure

The governance framework is organized around the following functions:

Project Founder

The Project Founder is responsible for:

- Establishing the project's vision and direction.
- Maintaining the core identity and principles.
- Making or authorizing major strategic decisions.
- Appointing individuals to authorized project responsibilities.
- Ensuring that public commitments are properly reviewed.

Project Management

Project management responsibilities may include:

- Coordinating approved activities.
- Managing operational priorities.
- Monitoring project progress.
- Reporting relevant developments.
- Ensuring that assigned tasks follow established standards.

These responsibilities may be assigned as the project develops.

Community Management

Community management is responsible for:

- Maintaining community standards.
- Supporting official communication.
- Coordinating moderator activities.
- Reporting security concerns.
- Escalating significant community issues.

Contributors and Team Members

Contributors and team members are expected to:

- Work within their assigned responsibilities.
- Follow official project guidelines.
- Protect confidential information.
- Report relevant risks and operational issues.
- Avoid making unauthorized official statements or commitments.

4. Decision-Making Authority

Decisions should be classified according to their impact.

Strategic Decisions

Examples include:

- Changes to the ecosystem's long-term direction.
- Introduction of new Sword Coins.
- Major changes to the brand identity.
- Significant changes to project commitments.

These decisions require authorization from the Project Founder or an explicitly designated authority.

Operational Decisions

Examples include:

- Routine community activities.
- Content publication within approved guidelines.
- Documentation maintenance.
- Daily moderation activities.

These decisions may be handled by authorized team members within their assigned responsibilities.

Security-Critical Decisions

Examples include:

- Wallet access changes.
- Account recovery procedures.
- Changes to security permissions.
- Response to suspected account compromise.

Security-critical decisions must be handled by authorized individuals using appropriate security procedures.

5. Authority Boundaries

No individual may:

- Claim official authority without authorization.
- Make financial or token-related commitments on behalf of the project without approval.
- Change official project policies independently.
- Access restricted systems or assets without permission.
- Use the MYBIE SWORD identity for unauthorized personal activities.

Delegated authority must have clearly defined responsibilities and limits.

6. Transparency and Reporting

MYBIE SWORD aims to maintain clear and responsible communication.

Relevant project information may include:

- Major project decisions.
- Official documentation updates.
- Community policy changes.
- Verified project developments.
- Relevant security announcements.

Confidential credentials, private keys, personal information, and sensitive operational details must never be disclosed publicly.

Transparency must not compromise security or privacy.

7. Accountability and Review

Project responsibilities should be reviewed periodically.

When a problem occurs:

1. Identify the issue.
2. Assess its potential impact.
3. Determine who is responsible for handling it.
4. Take appropriate corrective action.
5. Document significant decisions and outcomes where appropriate.

Misconduct, abuse of authority, or repeated violations may result in removal of assigned responsibilities or access.

8. Conflicts of Interest

Individuals acting on behalf of MYBIE SWORD should disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Personal financial interests must not be misrepresented as official project decisions.

Promotional activities, partnerships, and financial arrangements require appropriate authorization and transparent communication.

9. Governance Development

The governance framework may evolve as MYBIE SWORD grows.

Additional policies may be introduced when necessary, including:

- Treasury management policies.
- Wallet security procedures.
- Token distribution policies.
- Partnership approval procedures.
- Community voting mechanisms.
- Internal audit and reporting procedures.

Any future governance mechanism must be documented before being presented as an active project system.

10. Amendments

This framework may be reviewed and updated as the ecosystem develops.

Significant amendments should be documented and communicated through official project channels.

Previous versions should remain traceable through the project's documentation history.

11. Final Statement

MYBIE SWORD believes that authority must come with responsibility, decisions must have accountability, and trust must be supported by consistent actions.

A strong ecosystem requires more than a shared identity. It requires clear standards, responsible leadership, and disciplined governance.
