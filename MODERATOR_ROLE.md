⚔️ MYBIE SWORD — Moderator Role

1. Introduction

Moderators play an important role in maintaining a safe, organized, and active MYBIE SWORD community.

The Moderator Team is responsible for managing community interactions, maintaining official communication channels, and supporting community growth.

Moderators must act responsibly and follow the standards established by MYBIE SWORD.

2. Main Responsibilities

The Moderator Team is responsible for:

- Managing the official Telegram community.
- Managing the official X account.
- Welcoming new community members.
- Helping attract new members through responsible engagement.
- Answering community questions.
- Maintaining healthy discussions.
- Enforcing Community Guidelines.
- Removing spam and harmful content.
- Monitoring suspicious accounts and scam activities.
- Sharing verified project announcements.
- Reporting important community developments.

3. Community Management

Moderators must:

- Maintain a respectful and welcoming environment.
- Respond to members professionally.
- Encourage constructive discussions.
- Help resolve minor community conflicts.
- Prevent harassment, spam, and disruptive behavior.
- Apply community rules fairly and consistently.

4. Official X Account Management

When authorized to manage the official X account, moderators may:

- Publish approved project updates.
- Share official content and announcements.
- Respond to relevant community discussions.
- Engage with other legitimate communities.
- Help increase project visibility.
- Report impersonation and suspicious accounts.

Moderators must not publish unverified information or make unauthorized project commitments.

5. Security Responsibilities

Moderators must:

- Watch for scams, phishing, and impersonation.
- Report suspicious links and accounts.
- Warn members about security threats.
- Never request private keys or wallet seed phrases.
- Protect access to official accounts.
- Follow approved account security procedures.

Moderators must never request or hold project wallet private keys or seed phrases.

6. Communication Standards

Moderators are expected to:

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Provide accurate information.
- Avoid misleading statements.
- Never promise guaranteed financial returns.
- Distinguish personal opinions from official announcements.
- Refer sensitive or uncertain matters to the authorized project representative.

7. Authority and Limitations

Moderators are authorized to manage community activities within their assigned responsibilities.

However, moderators are not automatically authorized to:

- Make financial decisions for the project.
- Access or control project wallets.
- Change tokenomics or Smart Contract configurations.
- Announce unconfirmed listings or partnerships.
- Promise token prices or investment returns.
- Make official commitments on behalf of the developer without approval.

8. Reporting and Coordination

Moderators should report:

- Serious community conflicts.
- Scam attempts and security threats.
- Significant member complaints.
- Impersonation activities.
- Important community feedback.
- Issues requiring developer attention.

Important decisions and sensitive matters must be escalated to the authorized project representative.

9. Moderator Conduct

Every moderator must demonstrate:

- Integrity.
- Responsibility.
- Honesty.
- Respect.
- Consistency.
- Accountability.

Moderators must not abuse their authority, misuse community access, or use their position for personal financial gain.

10. Final Statement

Being a moderator is a responsibility, not merely a position.

Every moderator represents the standards and reputation of MYBIE SWORD through their actions and communication.

One House. Multiple Sword Coins. One Standard.

We have nothing to hide. Transparency is not an option; it is our standard.
