MYBIE SWORD is a project ecosystem built around a shared identity, vision, philosophy, and standards.

- Accountability

| Principle | Meaning |

## 6. Community & Team

A strong ecosystem requires responsible participation and effective teamwork.

The following are the **planned community functions**. They describe the intended structure and do not imply that all positions are currently staffed or active:

1. **Moderator Team** — Community management, official communication channels, member engagement, and community security.
2. **Content Creator Team** — Memes, graphics, videos, and creative materials.
3. **Marketing & Investor Relations Team** — Project awareness, external relationships, and accurate project communication.

These functions may be combined or introduced gradually according to project needs and available resources. Any assigned role must operate within clearly defined responsibilities and authority.

## 7. Transparency & Distribution

MYBIE SWORD has published the following distribution commitments for $MYBIE:

- No developer token purchases at launch.
- No private developer token reserve or allocation.
- No team, promoter, or insider allocation.
- No presale or hidden allocation.
- Open and transparent token distribution.
- Monitoring significant holder and wallet movements.
- Prioritizing project wallet and asset security.

These commitments must be reflected in the actual launch configuration and distribution process. Relevant details should be verified and made public when available.
