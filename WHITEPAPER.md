## 6. Security and Operational Standards

Security is an ongoing responsibility, not a guarantee. MYBIE SWORD's documented baseline includes:

- **Account protection:** unique passwords, multi-factor authentication where supported, and periodic session and permission reviews.
- **Least-privilege access:** each contributor receives only the permissions required for an assigned task.
- **Wallet separation:** wallet purposes, control, and recovery arrangements should be defined before project funds are held.
- **Secret protection:** seed phrases, private keys, passwords, and recovery codes must never be shared or stored in public repositories.
- **Change review:** material changes to accounts, wallets, token configuration, official links, or permissions should be approved and recorded.
- **Incident response:** suspected compromise should be contained promptly, evidence preserved safely, and affected community members warned through verified channels when necessary.

Publishing security procedures does not mean every control is already configured or tested. The project must not claim an audit, penetration test, custody certification, or absolute security without evidence.

## 7. Governance and Accountability

The governance framework defines intended decision boundaries and accountability expectations. It does not claim that a staffed board, binding community voting system, treasury committee, independent audit function, or formal legal entity currently exists.

The documented governance approach includes:

- Strategic decisions and material public commitments remain with the founder or a person explicitly delegated authority.
- Contributors may act only within their assigned scope and permissions.
- Financial, token-related, technical, and security-critical decisions require appropriate authorization and records.
- Material conflicts of interest, paid promotions, sponsorships, and referral benefits should be disclosed.
- Community feedback is welcome. Informal polls are advisory unless a formal voting process and its binding scope are separately published.

A role title alone does not grant access to project wallets, financial assets, critical accounts, or authority to make binding promises on behalf of MYBIE SWORD.

## 8. Community Principles

The community is expected to support constructive discussion and protect members from impersonation, phishing, and misleading claims.

Official administrators will not ask members for seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or recovery codes. Members should verify links through official channels and be cautious of unsolicited direct messages, fake support accounts, guaranteed-return claims, and requests to send funds.

Community rules and moderation procedures are published separately and may be updated as the project develops.

## 10. Risks and Limitations

Participation in cryptocurrency projects involves substantial risks, including but not limited to:

- **Market risk:** token prices can be highly volatile and may fall to zero.
- **Liquidity risk:** a holder may be unable to sell an asset at a desired price or at all.
- **Technical risk:** software, smart contracts, wallets, platforms, and blockchain infrastructure may contain vulnerabilities or fail.
- **Operational and security risk:** accounts, devices, keys, or communication channels may be compromised.
- **Regulatory and legal risk:** laws and regulatory treatment differ across jurisdictions and may change.
- **Execution risk:** planned features, launch steps, partnerships, or future projects may be delayed, changed, or never completed.
- **Information risk:** public information may be incomplete or become outdated; readers should verify material facts independently.

No statement in this paper removes these risks or guarantees that losses can be prevented or recovered.
